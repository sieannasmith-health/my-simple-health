Greek Chicken Bowl

Herb-marinated chicken, fluffy rice, crisp cucumber and tomatoes, kalamata olives, feta, and creamy homemade tzatziki come together in this fresh, Mediterranean-inspired bowl.

PREP 10 min
COOK 15 min
SERVES 2

Ingredients

CHICKEN

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

BOWL

  • 1 cup cooked rice or quinoa
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup cucumber, diced
  • ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup kalamata olives, pitted and halved
  • ¼ cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

TZATZIKI

  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh dill, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, basil, salt, and black pepper.

  2. Place the chicken in a shallow dish or zip-top bag and pour the marinade over it. Turn to coat and let marinate for 15 to 30 minutes.

  3. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 6 to 7 minutes per side, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F. Let rest for 5 minutes, then slice.

  4. While the chicken cooks, make the tzatziki. Combine the Greek yogurt, grated cucumber, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, dill, salt, and black pepper in a small bowl.

  5. Divide the rice or quinoa between two bowls. Top with sliced chicken, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, kalamata olives, and crumbled feta.

  6. Spoon the tzatziki alongside the bowl components or drizzle it over the top.

  7. Finish with fresh parsley and serve.

Simple tip.

Make extra chicken and rice while you are already cooking. Both can be repurposed for salads, wraps, or another quick bowl later in the week.

Make it your own.

Use quinoa instead of rice, add leafy greens, or include roasted red peppers or chickpeas. If you prefer more crunch, add a sprinkle of chopped cucumber just before serving.
