MY SIMPLE HEALTH ASSESSMENTS

Your Wellness Wheel

Take a few minutes to reflect on eight areas of your life. Your Wellness Wheel is a snapshot of how satisfied you feel with each area right now.

There are no passing or failing scores. This is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical or diagnostic assessment.

REFLECT ON WHERE YOU ARE

Wellness is multidimensional.

For each dimension, choose the number that best reflects how satisfied you feel with that area of your life today. Try to answer based on your current experience rather than where you think you should be.

ABOUT THIS TOOL

Reflection, not diagnosis.

The My Simple Health Wellness Wheel is designed for general wellness education and self-reflection. Your ratings are subjective and should not be interpreted as medical diagnoses, mental health diagnoses, or measures of clinical risk.
