Your Information

Understanding your health should not require giving up clarity about what happens to your information.

What belongs in My Health?

You decide what you intentionally add and explore. MSH may also create system observations or derived information when a feature requires it, but those should remain distinguishable from information you directly recorded.

Why does provenance matter?

Knowing where information came from helps prevent a prediction, estimate, AI interpretation, or personal observation from being mistaken for a directly recorded fact.

Can I correct information?

Where MSH presents a personal synthesis or interpretation, the product should provide a way to confirm, correct, or decline it rather than silently defining you.

Privacy and control

MSH is being designed so privacy choices, data use, and information controls can be inspected rather than assumed. As account and cloud-storage features expand, this support area will document the controls available to you.

This page describes the product approach. Formal privacy terms and any legally required disclosures remain available through the dedicated Privacy materials.
