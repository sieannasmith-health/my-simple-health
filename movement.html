MOVEMENT FOR REAL LIFE

You don't have to live at the gym to live an active life.

Physical activity can happen throughout your day. Walking, strength training, stretching, household activities, recreation, and intentional exercise can all contribute to an active lifestyle.

The goal is not perfection. It is finding realistic ways to move that fit your body, preferences, schedule, and life.

WHY MOVEMENT MATTERS

Movement supports more than physical fitness.

01

Heart Health

Regular physical activity supports cardiovascular health and overall physical function.

02

Strength

Strength-building activity helps support muscles, bones, mobility, and everyday independence.

03

Daily Function

Everyday movement helps maintain the physical capacity needed for daily life.

04

Wellbeing

Physical activity can also support mood, sleep, stress management, and overall wellbeing.
