How attribution works

Give people credit for the part only people can contribute.

Human contributionJudgment, decision, relationship, leadership, creation or review.
Agent contributionResearch, synthesis, implementation, analysis or preparation performed by an authorized worker.
Collaborative contributionA meaningful outcome produced through human + agent work.
EvidencePR, commit, test, decision, artifact or other durable proof.

From work to shared impact

Routing model
Objective→ Mission→ Agentic work→ Human review→ Evidence→ Impact→ Relatedness

When live GitHub/runtime integration is connected, qualifying evidence events should update the employee mission and Impact Portfolio and then publish an appropriate shared contribution event without duplicate manual entry.
