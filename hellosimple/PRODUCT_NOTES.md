# HelloSimple MVP product notes

## Daily operating principle

A person's formal role is relatively stable. Their daily work is dynamic. HelloSimple should refresh the employee's Today view from current MSH objectives, assigned workstreams, calendar commitments, human-only actions, and relevant Hello Worker activity.

The employee should always be able to answer:

1. What should I focus on today?
2. What is already being handled by AI?
3. Where is my judgment or action needed?
4. What skill am I practicing or demonstrating?
5. What company outcome does this move?
6. What evidence will be recorded when I finish?

## Progression principle

Use Whiteout Survival-style progression mechanics without game animation: XP, levels, chapters, milestone rewards, clear next goals, and responsibility unlocks. Rewards represent proven capability and meaningful contribution. They do not automatically grant sensitive permissions or company authority.

## Learning reference

Scribe-style procedural help should answer “how do I do this?” inside the mission. Guidde-style contextual guidance should answer “show me how this works” without forcing the employee to leave the work flow. Real work evidence, not training completion, advances demonstrated skill.
