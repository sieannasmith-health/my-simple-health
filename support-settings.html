Account & Settings

Personalize how My Simple Health feels without changing what your health information means.

Appearance

Use the appearance control in the My Health header to choose Light, Dark, or System. System follows your device appearance preference.

Sound

When environmental sound is available, the audio control appears with the other workspace controls in the upper-right area of the My Health header.

Personalization

Some areas may offer personal accent or display preferences. Personal styling is kept separate from semantic styling used to communicate evidence, provenance, warnings, estimates, predictions, or other meaning.

Accessibility

MSH aims to support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, responsive layouts, reduced-motion preferences, and clear controls. If an interface prevents you from using a feature, please report it.
