Steak & Sweet Potato Bowl

A hearty bowl filled with juicy steak, caramelized sweet potatoes, roasted broccoli and tomatoes, creamy avocado, and a bright garlic herb dressing. It is colorful, satisfying, and packed with texture.

PREP 10 min
COOK 20 min
SERVES 2

Ingredients

STEAK

  • 12 oz sirloin or flank steak
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

SWEET POTATOES

  • 1 large sweet potato, peeled and cubed
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

VEGGIES

  • 2 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

GARLIC HERB DRESSING

  • ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt or mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Water to thin, if needed

TO ASSEMBLE

  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons pepitas
  • Fresh parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Season the steak with garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper. Let it sit while you prepare the remaining ingredients.

  2. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

  3. Toss the sweet potato cubes with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Spread them on one side of the baking sheet.

  4. Toss the broccoli with olive oil, salt, and black pepper. Add the broccoli to the other side of the baking sheet along with the cherry tomatoes.

  5. Roast for 18 to 20 minutes, tossing halfway through, until the vegetables are tender and lightly caramelized.

  6. Meanwhile, heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add a small amount of olive oil if needed and cook the steak for about 4 to 5 minutes per side for medium-rare, or until it reaches your desired doneness. Remove from the pan and let rest for 5 minutes before slicing against the grain.

  7. In a small bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt or mayonnaise, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, parsley, Dijon mustard, salt, and black pepper. Add water a little at a time if needed to reach your preferred consistency.

  8. Divide the roasted sweet potatoes, broccoli, and tomatoes between two bowls. Top with sliced steak, avocado, and pepitas.

  9. Drizzle with garlic herb dressing, garnish with fresh parsley, and serve.

Simple tip.

Prep the sweet potatoes and steak ahead of time for quick, protein-packed bowls during the week. Store the dressing separately and add it just before serving.

Make it your own.

Swap broccoli for Brussels sprouts, add leafy greens beneath the roasted vegetables, or use quinoa or brown rice for a grain-based bowl. Feta also works well as an optional finishing touch.
