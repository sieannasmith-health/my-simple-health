ABOUT MY SIMPLE HEALTH

Understand your health.
Live your life.

My Simple Health brings health information, your experiences, and the context of your life together so you can build a clearer picture of your health over time.

01

THE MISSION

Health makes more sense when you can see the whole picture.

Health information often arrives in pieces: a symptom, a recommendation, a workout, a medication change, a stressful week, a research finding. My Simple Health is designed to help those pieces remain understandable without pretending they all mean the same thing.

The goal is not to turn your life into a health project. It is to give you a coherent place to notice, understand, explore, try, and learn from what matters to you.

02

HOW MSH WORKS

One connected health journey.

Landscape

Horizon

Path

Practice

Try something small enough to fit into real life.

Discovery

Journey

See how your experiences and understanding change over time.

03

OUR PRINCIPLES

Built on science. Designed for real life.

Evidence

See what the science supports and how strongly.

Context

Your health does not happen in isolation. Your life is part of the picture.

Uncertainty

We do not pretend to know what we do not. Uncertainty stays visible.

Privacy

Your health information is yours. You should know what happens to it.

Choice

You decide what belongs in My Health and what you want to explore.

A NOTE ABOUT HEALTH INFORMATION

Education and understanding, not individual medical care.

My Simple Health provides health education and tools for personal understanding. It does not diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, or replace professional medical care.
