ABOUT MY SIMPLE HEALTH

Health information for real life.

My Simple Health makes health information easier to understand, easier to use, and easier to fit into everyday life.

01

THE MISSION

Make healthy choices feel more understandable and realistic.

Health information can be overwhelming. Recommendations change, research can feel difficult to interpret, and everyday life rarely looks as simple as a checklist.

My Simple Health exists to translate evidence-informed health information into practical guidance people can actually use. The goal is not perfection. It is helping people understand their options, make informed choices, and build habits that support long-term health.

THE APPROACH

Evidence-informed, practical, and grounded in everyday life.

Understand the evidence.

Health guidance should be informed by credible research, public health recommendations, and the strength of the available evidence.

Translate it clearly.

Good information is not useful if people cannot understand what it means. My Simple Health focuses on clear explanations without unnecessary jargon.

Make it practical.

Health happens within real schedules, budgets, preferences, responsibilities, cultures, and circumstances.

Leave room for individuality.

There is rarely one perfect way to eat, move, sleep, or care for yourself. Health guidance should support informed choices rather than rigid rules.

BEHIND MY SIMPLE HEALTH

Hi, I'm Sieanna.

I created My Simple Health from a long-standing interest in health education, nutrition, physical activity, and public health.

My background in health science and epidemiology shapes the way I approach health information: understand the evidence, look at the larger population-health picture, and then translate what matters into something useful for everyday life.

My Simple Health is where those interests come together through health education, practical resources, recipes, and eventually original health-data insights.

A NOTE ABOUT HEALTH INFORMATION

Education, not individual medical care.

My Simple Health provides general health education and is not a substitute for individualized medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Health needs vary from person to person, so questions about your own health should be discussed with an appropriate healthcare professional.
