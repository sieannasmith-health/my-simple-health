Baja Fish Tacos

Crispy golden fish, crunchy citrusy slaw, creamy Baja sauce, fresh pico de gallo, and avocado come together in warm tortillas for bright, satisfying coastal-style tacos.

PREP 15 min
COOK 15 min
SERVES 4

Ingredients

FISH

  • 1 lb white fish, such as cod, mahi mahi, or tilapia
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup sparkling water or beer
  • Oil for frying, or cooking spray for baking or air frying

SLAW

  • 2 cups shredded green cabbage
  • 1 cup shredded purple cabbage
  • ¼ cup shredded carrots
  • 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

BAJA SAUCE

  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • ¼ cup sour cream or plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons hot sauce, to taste
  • 1 teaspoon lime juice
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt to taste

TO SERVE

  • 8 small corn or flour tortillas
  • Pico de gallo
  • Avocado or sliced avocado
  • Lime wedges
  • Fresh cilantro

Instructions

  1. Pat the fish dry and cut it into strips. In a bowl, whisk together the flour, cornstarch, chili powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper.

  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the egg and sparkling water or beer.

  3. Dip each fish strip into the wet mixture, then dredge it in the dry mixture, pressing lightly so the coating adheres.

  4. Heat about ½ inch of oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the fish in batches for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through. Drain on paper towels.

  5. For an alternative cooking method, bake the coated fish at 425°F for 15 to 18 minutes, or air fry at 400°F for 10 to 12 minutes, until crisp and cooked through.

  6. In a bowl, toss together the green cabbage, purple cabbage, carrots, cilantro, lime juice, and salt. Set aside.

  7. In a small bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, sour cream or Greek yogurt, hot sauce, lime juice, garlic powder, and salt until smooth.

  8. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or directly over a gas flame until soft and lightly charred.

  9. Assemble the tacos with slaw, crispy fish, Baja sauce, pico de gallo, avocado, and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges.

Simple tip.

Let the slaw chill for about 15 minutes before assembling the tacos. This gives the cabbage time to absorb the lime while still keeping its crunch.

Make it your own.

Use the frying, baking, or air-frying method depending on what works best for you. Adjust the hot sauce in the Baja sauce to control the heat, or add pickled onions or jalapeño for extra tang and spice.
