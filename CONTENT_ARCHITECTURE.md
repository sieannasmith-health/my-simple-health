# Public content architecture

## Decision

`Resources` is the single public destination for health education and practical health content.

`Topics` and `Blog` are legacy content groupings, not separate primary destinations.

## User-facing model

Resources may organize content by both topic and format.

### Topics

Examples: Nutrition, Movement, Sleep, Wellbeing, Prevention, Environment.

### Formats

- Learn: articles, explainers, evidence summaries
- Try: interactive activities
- Reflect: guided reflection
- Explore: assessments and self-exploration
- Use: guides, planners, checklists, calculators and practical tools

A resource can have a topic, a format, publication metadata, evidence/provenance metadata, and related resources without requiring a separate Blog or Topics destination.

## Navigation rule

Do not add `Topics` and `Blog` back to primary navigation. Link to `Resources` instead. Existing topic/article URLs may remain for backward compatibility while content is progressively consolidated.

## Content preservation

Do not delete existing articles or topic pages merely to simplify navigation. Preserve useful content and progressively surface it through Resources. Redirect legacy index pages only when their content has been safely represented in Resources.
