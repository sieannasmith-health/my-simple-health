My Health

My Health is your personal health workspace. It is designed to open into your health, not make you learn a complicated system first.

How does My Health work?

Information can enter My Health from things you record, activities you complete, connected experiences, and information the system can responsibly derive. MSH keeps those sources distinct so an observation does not quietly become a conclusion.

Landscape

Landscape brings multiple dimensions of health and life together. It is a current picture, not an overall health score.

Calendar

Calendar places health information in time so you can see what happened around an event, experience, symptom, practice, or change.

Discovery

Discovery is where observations can become personal learning. MSH preserves uncertainty when a connection is possible but not established.

Practice and Journey

Practice lets you try something in real life. Journey preserves what you notice, try, and learn so your picture can become richer over time.
