How My Simple Health is built

Built on science. Designed for real life. These principles guide how MSH handles your health, your information, and what we do not yet know.

Evidence

MSH distinguishes established knowledge from emerging evidence and makes sources inspectable when evidence matters.

Context

Your experiences, environment, routines, resources, priorities, and circumstances can shape health. Context helps build the picture without defining you.

Uncertainty

MSH does not need to pretend certainty where it does not exist. Known, observed, estimated, predicted, and unclear information should not all look the same.

Privacy

You should be able to understand what information is used, why it is used, and what choices you have over it.

Choice

MSH can support understanding without taking ownership of your health journey. You decide what to explore and what belongs in your health experience.
