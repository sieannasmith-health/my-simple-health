Mediterranean Chicken Grain Bowl

Bright lemon herb chicken, fluffy quinoa, crisp cucumber and tomatoes, briny olives, creamy hummus, and feta come together in this fresh Mediterranean bowl finished with a lemon tahini dressing.

PREP 10 min
COOK 20 min
SERVES 2

Ingredients

LEMON HERB CHICKEN

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

BOWL

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 cup cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup Kalamata olives, pitted and halved
  • ¼ cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons hummus

LEMON TAHINI DRESSING

  • 2 tablespoons tahini
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon water, plus more as needed
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, thyme, salt, and black pepper.

  2. Place the chicken in a shallow dish or zip-top bag and pour the marinade over it. Turn to coat and let marinate for at least 15 minutes, or up to 2 hours.

  3. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook for 5 to 6 minutes per side, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F. Remove from the heat and let rest for 5 minutes, then slice.

  4. In a small bowl, whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, salt, and black pepper. Add water a little at a time until the dressing reaches your preferred consistency.

  5. Divide the cooked quinoa between two bowls. Top with sliced chicken, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, Kalamata olives, feta, hummus, and fresh parsley.

  6. Drizzle with lemon tahini dressing and serve.

Simple tip.

Make extra quinoa and chicken while you are already cooking. Store them separately so you can assemble another bowl quickly later in the week.

Make it your own.

Add leafy greens, roasted red peppers, chickpeas, or avocado. You can also swap quinoa for brown rice or another grain and adjust the tahini dressing with extra lemon or water to taste.
