SLEEP FOR REAL LIFE

Better sleep doesn't require a perfect bedtime routine.

Healthy sleep is influenced by more than what happens during the final few minutes before bed.

Your schedule, light exposure, physical activity, caffeine timing, bedroom environment, and opportunities for rest can all influence the conditions surrounding sleep.

WHY SLEEP MATTERS

Sleep supports your whole body.

01

Physical Health

Healthy sleep supports cardiovascular, metabolic, immune, and overall physical health.

02

Brain Function

Sleep supports attention, learning, memory, decision-making, and daily functioning.

03

Recovery

Sleep gives your body regular opportunities for physical and mental recovery.

04

Wellbeing

Healthy sleep can support mood, emotional regulation, energy, and quality of life.
