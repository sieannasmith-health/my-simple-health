Chicken Tinga Tacos

Juicy shredded chicken simmered in a smoky chipotle tomato sauce with onions and garlic, then piled into warm tortillas with crisp vegetables, cheese, cilantro, and lime.

PREP 15 min
COOK 25 min
SERVES 4

Ingredients

CHICKEN TINGA

  • 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, minced
  • 2 teaspoons adobo sauce
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • ½ teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • Fresh cilantro, for garnish

TO SERVE

  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • Shredded lettuce or cabbage
  • Pickled red onions or diced red onion
  • Cotija cheese or queso fresco
  • Pico de gallo
  • Lime wedges
  • Sliced avocado, optional
  • Hot sauce, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced onion and cook for 5 to 6 minutes, until softened.

  2. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.

  3. Add the fire-roasted tomatoes, chipotle peppers, adobo sauce, cumin, oregano, salt, black pepper, chicken broth, and lime juice. Stir to combine.

  4. Add the chicken breasts to the skillet. Bring the mixture to a simmer, cover, and cook for 15 to 18 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and tender.

  5. Remove the chicken from the skillet and shred it with two forks.

  6. Return the shredded chicken to the sauce and simmer uncovered for 5 to 7 minutes, until the sauce thickens slightly and coats the chicken. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.

  7. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or directly over a gas flame for a few seconds per side until pliable and lightly charred.

  8. Assemble the tacos with chicken tinga and your favorite toppings. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve with lime wedges.

Simple tip.

Make extra chicken tinga while you are already cooking. The smoky shredded chicken is also great in burrito bowls, quesadillas, tostadas, or over greens.

Make it your own.

Use extra chipotle or adobo sauce for more heat, or keep it milder with less. Add avocado, pickled onions, queso fresco, pico de gallo, or shredded cabbage depending on what you have on hand.
