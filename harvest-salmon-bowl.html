Harvest Salmon Bowl

A nourishing bowl built around maple-Dijon salmon, quinoa, roasted sweet potatoes, fresh vegetables, dried cranberries, pumpkin seeds, and creamy goat cheese. A sweet and tangy maple Dijon vinaigrette ties all of the harvest flavors together.

PREP 10 min
COOK 20 min
SERVES 2

Ingredients

SALMON

  • 2 (5 to 6 oz) salmon fillets
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • ½ teaspoon dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

BOWL

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 cup roasted sweet potato cubes
  • 1 cup baby spinach or arugula
  • ½ cup cucumber, diced
  • ½ cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ cup red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons dried cranberries
  • 2 tablespoons pumpkin seeds
  • 2 tablespoons crumbled goat cheese
  • 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

MAPLE DIJON VINAIGRETTE

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, maple syrup, Dijon mustard, garlic, thyme, salt, and black pepper.

  3. Place the salmon fillets on the prepared baking sheet and brush with half of the maple-Dijon mixture.

  4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the salmon flakes easily with a fork. Brush with the remaining glaze halfway through cooking.

  5. While the salmon cooks, prepare the quinoa according to package directions if it is not already cooked. Warm the roasted sweet potatoes if needed.

  6. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, maple syrup, Dijon mustard, apple cider vinegar, salt, and black pepper for the vinaigrette.

  7. Divide the spinach or arugula between two bowls. Add the quinoa, roasted sweet potatoes, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, dried cranberries, and pumpkin seeds.

  8. Place a salmon fillet on top of each bowl. Sprinkle with goat cheese and fresh parsley, then drizzle with the maple Dijon vinaigrette.

  9. Serve warm and enjoy.

Simple tip.

Make a double batch of roasted sweet potatoes and quinoa so you already have the base of another bowl ready for later in the week.

Make it your own.

Swap goat cheese for feta, use kale instead of spinach or arugula, or substitute roasted butternut squash for the sweet potato. Walnuts or pecans also work well in place of pumpkin seeds.
