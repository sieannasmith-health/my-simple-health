AI in My Simple Health

MSH uses AI as an interface-aware intelligence layer to help explain, connect, and navigate health information while preserving evidence and uncertainty.

What can it help with?

Depending on the page and available context, AI can help explain information, clarify what you are seeing, support reflection, help navigate MSH, and connect relevant information without requiring you to restate everything on every screen.

What does it know about the page?

MSH can provide relevant interface context, such as the activity you are using or an item you selected. Context should be purposeful and limited to what helps the interaction.

Does AI diagnose me?

No. AI in MSH is not a diagnostic system and should not present uncertain interpretations as medical facts.

How are AI observations handled?

An AI-generated interpretation should remain distinguishable from something you recorded, an established scientific finding, or a confirmed personal conclusion. Where appropriate, you should be able to correct or reject a synthesis.

What if AI is unavailable?

The rest of My Health should remain usable. An AI service failure should not prevent you from navigating or accessing information that does not depend on that service.

For urgent or emergency medical concerns, use appropriate emergency or clinical services rather than relying on MSH.
