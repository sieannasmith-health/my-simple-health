The intelligence is in the usefulness—not the performance of being AI.

TRUST, BUILT INTO THE SYSTEM

You should be able to understand what MSH knows—and why.

Privacy, provenance, evidence, permissions, and accessibility should be inspectable parts of the product—not generic reassurance at the bottom of a page.

EVIDENCEWhat supports this?
