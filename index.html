TWO KINDS OF KNOWING

You are the expert on your life.

Science has expertise. Health professionals have expertise. You have expertise too.

You know what you experience, what matters to you, what you've tried, what fits your life, what doesn't, and when someone else's categories don't quite describe what you mean.

Evidence matters. So does your experience.

My Simple Health gives them somewhere to meet without pretending they are the same kind of knowledge.

EVIDENCE WITH HUMILITY

Built to respect what we know.
And what we don't.

Evidence has strength.

Not every finding deserves the same confidence.

Association isn't causation.

Things happening together doesn't automatically mean one caused the other.

Context matters.

Health is shaped by biology, behavior, environment, resources, responsibilities, relationships, access, and time.

Your observations are valuable.

Your experience can be preserved without automatically turning it into a scientific conclusion.

YOUR HEALTH. YOUR LIFE.

Make peace with your health.

You don't need to optimize every part of yourself. You don't need to have everything figured out. And you don't need another perfect plan.
